How It Works

From first call to live site, in days.

No technical knowledge needed. We handle the design, the hosting, and the upkeep. You run your business.

1

Tell us about your business

A short conversation (phone, video, or email) about your business, your customers, and what's not working with your current site. Have a logo, brand colors, or photos? Great. If not, we'll work with what you have.

A quick conversation
2

We design your custom site

We build a site from scratch around your business: your services, your story, your real photos. No templates, no cookie-cutter layouts.

First draft in a few business days
3

Review and refine

Tell us what you'd like changed. The easiest way: record a quick screen share with your voice (we recommend the free tool Loom) walking through the site, or just type it up. We revise until you love it.

Refined together until it's right
4

Go live

Once you approve it, we point your domain at the new site. You always keep full ownership of your domain name. Hosting, security, and SSL are set up and handled by us.

We handle the launch
5

Ongoing updates

Need new hours, prices, photos, or a new service added? Send us a note or a quick recording and we'll take care of it.

Most updates in 1–2 business days
